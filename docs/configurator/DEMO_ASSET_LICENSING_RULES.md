# Demo 3D Asset Licensing Rules

## Temporary demonstrator

The first public Configurator demonstrator may use a fictional vehicle identity such as **Aureon GT**.

The fictional name is a product/UI identity only. It must not imply OEM affiliation.

## Asset requirements

A candidate 3D asset may be integrated only when its license/evidence explicitly permits the intended use:

- commercial website/application use;
- WebGL/browser deployment;
- modification/adaptation when branding or geometry needs to be changed;
- use of the resulting asset inside the application;
- no prohibited redistribution of the source asset.

## Branding

Removing a third-party logo is permitted only when the asset license and applicable rights allow modification.

**Never remove branding solely to bypass an Editorial Use Only restriction.**

If the source license prohibits commercial use, modification, or branded-asset transformation, the candidate is rejected.

## Application presentation

The demo should display:

- Aureon GT
- Concept / Demonstrator
- Auto AI India Configurator

It must not display:

- Porsche, Panamera, or another third-party OEM/model name;
- third-party OEM logos or badges;
- wording suggesting OEM sponsorship or authorization.

## Production catalog boundary

This demo does not populate the authoritative `autoai` vehicle catalog.

A future verified OEM vehicle must enter through the normal production catalog, provenance, licensing, asset validation, publication, compatibility, and pricing gates.
